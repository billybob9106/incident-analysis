## Summary

The February 2026 IT incident analysis has been completed across 150 incidents, 309 remediation items (RIs), and 30 defined controls.

The programme is rated **AMBER**. Two specific areas require prompt attention from leadership:

**1. Control coverage gaps are unacceptable.** 8 controls have no remediation items mapped to them at all — meaning if an incident triggers those controls, there is no documented response. The highest-priority gaps are Backup Verification (CTL-017) and CAB Approval Mandatory (CTL-025).

**2. Open RI backlog is at 49%.** Nearly half of all remediation items remain open. This level of outstanding work creates compounding risk exposure and indicates that triage and closure processes need stronger governance.

On the positive side, incident volume is broadly stable and evenly distributed across categories, and P1 incidents represent only a small proportion of the total.
